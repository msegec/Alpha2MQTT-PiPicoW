v1.29 (fork, msegec/Alpha2MQTT-PiPicoW, 21 October 2026)

Firmware changes since v1.28.

Dispatch
	- set/charge and set/discharge write the whole dispatch block 0x0880 to 0x0888 as one Write Data
	  Register frame, about 50 ms on the bus instead of five writes two seconds apart. The response
	  reports "frames": 1 or 5.
	- An inverter that refuses the one frame write gets the five single writes; once those land after a
	  refused frame, later commands skip the frame until a reboot. An exception both paths get leaves
	  the one frame write in use.
	- Power-only writes are allowed for half the lease instead of a third.
	- The cached dispatch block is forgotten once the fast state reads dispatch stopped, so the next
	  command writes the whole block instead of a power write the inverter would ignore.
	- A set/charge or set/discharge over 32000 W or over 100 % SOC is refused and nothing is written.
	- An invalid set/charge or set/discharge is answered with its seq and ms, and failureDetail says
	  missing or range.
	- set/normal reports success only when the write succeeded.
	- The full block write, its fallback and the refused frame latch live in writeFullDispatchBlock.

Idle hold
	- Alpha2MQTT subscribes to homeassistant/status. Three minutes after Home Assistant's offline will,
	  or three minutes off the broker, the battery is held idle: a full dispatch block every 60 s,
	  charge 0 W to 100 % SOC for 120 s. An online or any set request inside the three minutes cancels
	  the pending will and nothing is written. When Home Assistant is back, a hold the Pico wrote is
	  ended at once with the set/normal write. A failed hold or release is tried again after 10 s.
	- The fast state gains haIdle: 1 while Home Assistant counts as gone.

RS485
	- Each reply is matched to its request: register count, echoed address and count, or the request's
	  own function code. A late reply from an earlier read is no longer taken as this one. After a
	  failed exchange the next flush waits for 10 ms of quiet, up to 150 ms.
	- A read reply or a read/register/raw request that would overrun the receive buffer is refused.
	- The rawData list is built by one bounded helper, so a long reply cannot overrun its buffer.
	- The unused fallback response built on every read is gone.

Robustness
	- Request keys and values over 31 characters are rejected instead of overrunning the parser's
	  buffers.
	- checkTimer uses unsigned elapsed time, so the schedules keep their phase across the millis() wrap
	  every 49.7 days and after a late run.
	- WiFi and MQTT reconnects return instead of waiting: WiFi.begin is reissued every 10 s and one
	  broker attempt is made every 5 s, so the loop, OTA and the dispatch work keep running through an
	  outage.
	- The Pico W reboots through its hardware watchdog, 8 s, when the loop hangs. It is fed in every
	  RS485 exchange, publish, broker attempt and OTA progress report.
	- The display refresh no longer services MQTT, so mqttCallback cannot run inside a half built
	  schedule payload.

Memory and bus time
	- The schedule tables hold register addresses only; the names come from the register read. RAM in
	  use drops by about 20 KB.
	- The display shows the fast state's last readings instead of reading them again: no bus traffic
	  for the display outside dispatch, two reads instead of five in it.
	- The load and total solar custom registers share one read of the PV strings.
	- Dead variables, no-op statements and empty placeholder blocks are deleted.

Tests
	- tests/firmware/run covers Dispatch.h, Timer.h, ModbusFrame.h, PvMeter.h and IdleHold.h.
